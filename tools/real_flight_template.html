What this shows (and doesn't)

  • Trajectory is PX4's own local-position estimate (cuVSLAM/EV-fed), not independent ground truth — if the estimator drifted, this plot drifts with it.
  • *Camera clearance is the nearest valid return in the depth frame closest in time, minus a placeholder drone radius — what the camera reported, not a ground-truth distance to the course geometry.
  • Course-frame position assumes the PX4 local-position origin is the launch mark and the drone launched facing +Y (into the first corridor) — same assumption used throughout this repo's route planning.
  • Depth/RGB thumbnails are small and lossily brightness-coded for a quick look, not calibrated imagery.
