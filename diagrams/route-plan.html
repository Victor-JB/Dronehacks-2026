Assumptions & caveats

  • Verified in local_sim: runs the real refnav.core.Mission code against this exact route and the real course geometry — completes 26/26 legs, no false stops, no deadlocks. Not yet verified against the FlyX Lab sim or real hardware.
  • Launch heading confirmed: drone faces +Y (into the first corridor) on the mark.
  • Gate B, not the window: C2a's window cutout wasn't extracted from the mesh, so this path swings around it instead of through it (window clears +5 bonus pts).
  • Tightest point is leg 17 (dashed circle, near the tunnel exit): ~33 cm raw clearance to a C1-chicane-wall corner, only 7.7 cm past a 25 cm placeholder drone radius — replace that placeholder with the real airframe span once the kit's in hand and recheck this corner specifically.
  • corridor_half_m/stop_m/lead_max_m/min_corridor_coverage are tuned down from the mission's defaults to fit this maze's ~1.07 m corridors — see local_sim/README.md for why, including a real design trade-off (camera mount angle vs. how conservative the coverage check can be).
  • Path assumes the sim/physical course matches this published CAD exactly; re-verify after any layout change notice.
