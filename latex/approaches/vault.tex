\section{Wall vault}
\textbf{Idea.} When blocked, climb over the obstacle instead of landing.

\textbf{How.} On branch \texttt{hardcoded-route-draft} (Rohan), \texttt{Mission.\_vault}.
Phases: approach to \texttt{vault\_standoff\_m}, climb until depth no longer sees the wall
(capped at \texttt{vault\_max\_alt\_m}), fly \texttt{vault\_overstep\_m} forward, then land.
Triggered by \texttt{vault\_on\_obstacle} or \texttt{vault\_only}.

\textbf{Assumptions / risks.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Must be allowed by the course rules.
  \item With \texttt{vault\_on\_obstacle}, any obstacle on the route triggers it, not only walls meant to be crossed.
  \item It ends the mission (lands) rather than rejoining the route.
\end{itemize}
