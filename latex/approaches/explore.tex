\section{Open-space explore}
\textbf{Idea.} No map. After climbing, repeatedly steer toward the most open direction in the depth image.

\textbf{How.} \texttt{Mission.\_explore} in \texttt{refnav/core.py}, enabled with
\texttt{"explore": true} in \texttt{route.json}. Each depth frame scores
\texttt{explore\_bearings} headings across $\pm$\texttt{explore\_half\_fov\_deg}:
\[
  \text{score}(b) = \min(\text{clearance}(b),\ \text{explore\_range\_m}) - \text{explore\_straight\_bias}\cdot|b|
\]
and turns toward the best one. Lands after \texttt{explore\_s}.

\textbf{Assumptions / risks.} Only sees inside the camera's field of view, so it can loop or
get stuck in dead ends. Has no notion of a goal.

\textbf{Sim results.} None yet.
