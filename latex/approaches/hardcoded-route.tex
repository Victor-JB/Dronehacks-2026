\section{Hardcoded route}
\textbf{Idea.} Plan offline from the course CAD and fly fixed waypoints.

\textbf{How.} On branch \texttt{hardcoded-route-draft} (Aryan). Waypoints were found with A*
over an occupancy grid built from \texttt{dronehacks-course-v3.obj}, walls inflated by
30\,cm. 20 waypoints from the launch mark to the landing pad through Gate~B; the window
bonus is not used. Plotted in \texttt{diagrams/route-plan.html}.

\textbf{Assumptions / risks.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The launch heading faces $+Y$ in the CAD frame. Unverified; if wrong, every waypoint is rotated.
  \item The sim maze matches the CAD. Unverified.
  \item 30\,cm margin vs.\ the real airframe span. Unverified.
  \item Open loop: any VIO drift accumulates over the whole route.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Sim results.} None yet.
